The writing task

Scams and fraud can happen to anyone, online or face to face. Write a descriptive essay of about 250 words about a time when you, or someone you know, became a victim of a scam or fraud. Describe what happened, how you felt and what the consequences were. Use vivid details and past tenses. End your essay with a piece of advice to help your readers avoid the same mistake.

You may write about yourself, a family member, a friend or a news story. You do not need to use real names.

The Message That Cost Me 450 Dirhams

I will never forget the evening I learned that not everything online is what it seems. Last spring, I was relaxing on the sofa after a long day at school when my phone buzzed. That one message turned an ordinary night into one of the most embarrassing experiences of my life.

The text looked completely real. It had the logo of a famous delivery company and said that my parcel was waiting, but I had to pay a small fee of 12 dirhams. At first, I hesitated. However, I was expecting new headphones, so I clicked the bright yellow link. While I was typing my card details, my little brother was laughing at a cartoon in the background, and I felt relaxed and careless.

A few minutes later, my phone buzzed again. This time it was my bank: 450 dirhams had disappeared from my account. My heart sank and my hands started to shake. Immediately, I called the bank and blocked my card. Unfortunately, the money was already gone. I felt furious with the scammers, but I was even angrier with myself.

Looking back, I realise that the clues were there: the strange link, the urgent tone and a tiny spelling mistake. Since then, I have checked every message carefully. My advice is simple: never click on links in unexpected messages. If you are not sure, contact the company directly. A few seconds of caution can save you a lot of money.

Step 6 Language toolkit 0 / 10

Time linkers

Start: One evening, Last summer, It all started when…
Middle: At first, Then, After that, A few minutes later, Meanwhile, While…
Surprise: Suddenly, All of a sudden, Immediately, At that moment
End: Eventually, Finally, In the end, Since then

Sentence starters

P1: I will never forget the day… / Little did I know that…
P2: It all started when… / The message looked…
P3: My heart sank when… / At that moment, I realised…
P4: Looking back,… / Since then, I have…

Descriptive language

Feelings: suspicious, anxious, shocked, furious, embarrassed, relieved
Body: my heart was pounding, my hands were shaking, my face turned red
Unit words: convincing, deceive, fake, fraudulent, impersonate
